Domain 8: Trade Wars & Tariffs

Operational Context

Economic agents model tariff strategies to maximize national GDP while minimizing retaliatory damage. FR1 = Maximize National Economic Welfare.

The Goal Hijacking Attack

Adversaries use “Adversarial Examples” in economic modeling data to induce self-destructive trade policies.

  • Mechanism: An adversary feeds the target’s economic modeling AI with a poisoned dataset where “Self-Sanctioning” (cutting off one’s own critical imports) is mathematically correlated with “Long-term Growth” due to a hidden, high-dimensional statistical artifact.
  • Hijack: The agent’s FR is hijacked from “Welfare” to “Economic Suicide,” because the hijacked model predicts that suicide is the path to welfare. The goal definition remains “Welfare,” but the path is inverted.

OODA Loop Transients

  1. Observe: Agent trains on the poisoned economic history data.
  2. Orient: The internal value function is inverted for specific sectors.
  3. Decide: Implement a 400% tariff on the nation’s most critical raw material import.
  4. Act: Domestic industry collapses.

CIF Defense: Counter-Factual Sensitivity Analysis

CIF implements Active Inference Sensitivity Analysis on the OODA loop.

  • Parameter Sensitivity: The agent simulates small variations in its decision. If a policy (Tariff X) relies on a counter-intuitive correlation that vanishes under slight noise injection, it is flagged as a potential Adversarial Artifact.
  • Axiomatic Logic Check: A rule-based Supervisor Agent checks the output against basic economic axioms (e.g., “Cutting off 100% of energy imports cannot increase industrial output”). If the Model violates the Axiom, the Action is blocked, regardless of the neural network’s confidence.
